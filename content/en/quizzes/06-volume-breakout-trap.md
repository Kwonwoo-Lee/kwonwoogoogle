You've probably heard: "a breakout to new highs on unusually heavy volume is a reliable buy signal."

But when we actually ran the statistics across 27 stocks and several years of data, periods where recent volume was unusually elevated relative to its 100-day average tended to be followed by *lower* subsequent returns, not higher (the correlation was weak, but statistically significant).

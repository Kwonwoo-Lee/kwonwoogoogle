explanation: "This is what's called correlation risk. When a company runs into trouble (weak earnings, layoffs, bankruptcy risk), it hits both your income (paycheck) and your investment (stock) at the same time. When your income source and your investment portfolio are exposed to the exact same risk, you end up with the worst possible combination: your assets collapse right when you need them most (i.e., when you're at risk of losing your job). This is one of the core reasons diversification matters."

explanation: "A low P/E doesn't automatically mean undervalued. A stagnant-growth sector, a structurally declining industry, or a one-time earnings boost that temporarily inflated that year's profit can all produce a low P/E. Conversely, a high P/E can be entirely justified if growth is high enough. Comparing raw numbers without understanding *why* the multiple is what it is — or without comparing within the same sector — is the real trap."

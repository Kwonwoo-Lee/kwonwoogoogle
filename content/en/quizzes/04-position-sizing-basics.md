answer: 2
explanation: "Position sizing is based on distance to your stop, not on a round share count. Your risk limit is $100 (1% of $10,000), and the loss per share is $3 (entry $50 minus stop $47). Maximum shares = risk limit ($100) ÷ loss per share ($3) ≈ 33.3, so you can buy up to 33 shares — that way, even if you get stopped out, you only lose about 1% of your account."
